<b>For the footbag community</b>

Footbag Network connects footbag players worldwide — freestyle, net, and casual. Find people to play with, keep up with what's happening in the scene, and talk shop about shoes, bags, and tricks.

<b>One community, not another silo</b>

The chat here is the same network the footbag community already uses, so you are not starting over in an empty app. Footbag Network is built on the open Matrix protocol and works with any other Matrix client, including Element, Cinny, and NeoChat.

<b>Easy to use</b>

Install, sign in, and start talking. No invite codes, no algorithm deciding what you see.

<b>Secure</b>

End-to-end encryption with encrypted message backups and automated key verification, so your private conversations stay private.

<b>Feature rich</b>

Group chats, spaces, images and files, voice messages, location sharing, custom stickers, and moderation tools for people running their own club spaces.

<b>Open source</b>

Footbag Network is free and open source under the AGPL. It is a fork of FluffyChat, an excellent Matrix client built by volunteers — our thanks to them. Source code and issue tracker: https://github.com/gwbischof/footbag-network
